Free Local AI Setup

Every simple AI job costs money if I always use the paid service. So I set it up so the simple jobs run on free tools, and the paid AI is saved for the hard thinking.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants to cut AI costs without losing quality on the hard tasks.

What I built

  • A local model called gemma4:26b, running on my own PC through Ollama (a program that runs AI models on your computer). It works offline with no usage limit.
  • A free hosted model from NVIDIA's trial, with 82 free models to choose from.
  • A connector called claude-sidekick that lets Claude call the local model for drafting, summaries and simple code.

Proof

  • I confirmed all three pieces worked on 2026-09-17.

Where it stands
