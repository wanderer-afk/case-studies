Ai invoice - 60-second video script

Goal: show a stranger, in 60 seconds, what the tool does and why an owner would care. Screen recording plus your voice. Nothing is sent or published by the demo.

Shot list (what is on screen)

Time On screen Voice (say this)
0-8 s Desk, then the morning list in a window "Every morning, this tells you who owes you money."
8-20 s Run RUN-DEMO.cmd, show the "Who owes me" list: 5 unpaid, $6,600, oldest 41 days "Five customers owe me $6,600. The oldest is 41 days late."
20-35 s Show the Drafts folder with the reminder for Riverside Dental "The reminder is already written in my words. I read it and hit send. It never sends it for me."
35-48 s Run aiinvoice paid INV-1041, then the morning run again "I mark one paid. It disappears from the list and never gets chased twice."
48-60 s Title card: "Ai invoice - a free tool that runs on your PC" "Built for owners who invoice from Word or Excel. Done for you for $99 to start."

How to record (Windows, free)

  1. Press Win + Alt + R to start the built-in Game Bar recording. Press it again to stop.
  2. Record one take at a time. Keep it under 60 seconds.
  3. Use only the demo folder. Do not show your real invoices or your name in a file path.

Before you record

  • Run RUN-DEMO.cmd once to check it works on your PC. Note: it has been run once here and the output matched the shot list above.
  • Close anything private from the screen (email, bank, the Tower dashboard).
