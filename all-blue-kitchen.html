All Blue Kitchen

Recipe books often skip how a dish really gets made. I wanted to learn each dish properly first: where it comes from, why each step works, and how hard it is. Then I write the recipe.

Who it's for

Home cooks who want to know why a recipe works, and anyone who wants a cookbook that shows its reasoning.

What I built

A cookbook project with my partner and Claude. Sanji is the Claude helper I set up as head chef, and he does the research. The project has: - Breakdowns of famous dishes, with a confidence note on each claim. - A difficulty score out of 13 for each dish, with the reason. - An ingredient and technique book that grows each time we research something. - A recipe format that marks every recipe UNTESTED until someone has cooked it. - A web book called "Single Star", with a contents page and chapters that turn like pages.

Proof

  • The breakdowns, the 13-point scale and the Michelin 50 list are all written.
  • The web book has ten chapters in progress.
  • Screenshot still to add: one finished recipe page.

Where it stands

In progress. Recipes are written, but most are still UNTESTED. One recipe has been cooked so far.

What I'd change

  • Recipes need real cooking tests before they count as tested.
  • Every chapter needs a clear test plan, so the book doesn't grow faster than the cooking.
